Questions

Good to know before you order.

Do you install machines outside China?

Yes. Our engineers travel for installation, commissioning and training. Scheduling is agreed with the order.

What does the warranty cover?

One year from commissioning. Damaged parts are replaced free during warranty; wear parts and damage from improper use are excluded.

Can a machine be customised?

Yes. Infeed conveyors, bag formers, feeders and pack counts are configured around your product and line.

How do we get a quote?

Send product dimensions, film type, pack count and target speed through the contact form. We will reply with a configuration.
