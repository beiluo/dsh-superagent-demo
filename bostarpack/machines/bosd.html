Built-in details

What the machine gives you

  • Centre sealing with edge trimming or electrostatic film control
  • Twin end sealers for high-speed production
  • Servo-driven film feed with PLC and touch-screen HMI
  • Misplaced-item skip (MIS) protects film and product
  • Quick-reset end-sealer cutter by manual handle
  • Two-metre shrink tunnel with adjustable temperature and airflow
  • Frequency-controlled conveyor speed
