Built-in details

What the machine gives you

  • Food-grade SUS304 stainless steel frame
  • Three servo motors; no product, no pack
  • Tilted plate keeps crumbs and ice clear of the product
  • Photo-cell sensor with auto tracking for cutting position
  • 10 stored recipes for fast changeovers
  • Misplaced-item skip (MIS) and quick-reset cutter
  • CE-compliant safety guarding and emergency stop
